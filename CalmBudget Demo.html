Getting started

A quick tour of the planner, plus the three settings that shape every number in it. Changes save themselves as you go.

Your currency

Starting balance

Money already in your account on the day you begin. It is added once, to your first month, so Safe to Spend starts from the truth.

Roll over leftover money

When a month ends with money unspent, carry it into the next month's income instead of losing sight of it.

Leftover carried into this month

The amount last month handed over. It only counts while roll-over is switched on.

How each section works

One plain line per screen — the whole system in a minute.
Home

One month on one screen: what came in, what went out, what is still safe to spend, plus charts that show where the money went.

Today

A short daily glance — total saved, total owed, this month's spending room, and whatever is coming up next.

Calendar

Tap any day to add a note, a bill, a payday, a debt payment or a savings move. Entries can repeat, and each one can count straight into your income, an envelope, a debt or a savings fund.

Goals

The bigger aims behind the numbers. Tap a goal's status pill to move it from not started, to in progress, to done.

Savings

Separate funds for separate purposes, each with its own progress bar and a pace hint so you know what to set aside each month.

Debt

Everything owed in one list. Sort by highest interest or smallest balance, and see a payoff bar and an estimated clear-by date for each debt.

Envelopes

One envelope per kind of spending, month by month. Log income and spending here, add debt payments and savings contributions, then check the yearly review and the full transaction log at the bottom.

Settings

Your name, your colourway, night mode, backups, and the option to archive a finished year and start fresh.
